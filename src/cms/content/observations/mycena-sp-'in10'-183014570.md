#367

- Growing scattered on fallen brown-rotted hardwood log in low oak/hickory woodland.
- Caps campanulate to umbonate with  radial striations, brown in color fading to tan towards margin.

- Lamellae adnate to succeeding unequal, white and shallow, with prominent cross-veins.
- Stipe smooth, simi-translucent, hollow, tough, fibrous, with bulbous base.

Additional Info
- KOH negative to yellowish on pileipellis.
- Spore print: white (or lightly colored).
