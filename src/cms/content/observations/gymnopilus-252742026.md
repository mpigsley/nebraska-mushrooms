- Growing on large fallen, decorticated hardwood log (presumably oak) in upland mixed oak/hickory woodland. 
- Smell: not distinctive
- Taste: very bitter
- KOH: wine red on pileipellis.
- Spore Print: rusty orange
- UV: bright yellow on lamellae under 365nm.
