E.B		“Death Valley”. Low, shaded.
E.S		well-decomposed broadleaf log.

P.T		fibrillose/y -> scaly/+
P.#		red
L.#(UV@365nm)	light greenish yellow.
S.#		yellow
VA.S	cortina-like

> Growing from well-decomposed broadleaf log in “Death Valley” in low, shaded biome.
Pileus red and fibrillose when young becoming scaly with age. Cortina-like annulus. Stipe yellow. UV: primarily gills fluorescing light greenish yellow under 365nm.
