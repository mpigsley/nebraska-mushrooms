AA35

- Growing on treated railroad tie in low, moist woodland area, near creek.
- Smell: not distinctive.
- Taste: very bitter.
- Bluing on cap surface.
- Microscopy: rehydrated in KOH and mounted in Melzer's Reagent. Basidia with 2 sterigmata. Bowling pin-shaped cystidia.
